After the Mint

Hold & Trading

AutoMuse transfers stay locked while the mint runs. They unlock after the PancakeSwap pool is created and the owner opens trading — the same rule for every holder, including the earliest minters. There is no staking program and no reward promise: what you hold is a fixed-supply token whose entire supply rule is written in one verified contract.
